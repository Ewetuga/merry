About Us

Merryjay Engineering Limited is an indigenous Nigerian powerhouse in engineering, construction, and technical services. For over a decade, we have built our reputation on precision,delivering complex electrical/mechanical installations and civil projects across Ota, Lagos, and beyond.

Located in a serene environment, MerryJay Event Place provides luxurious rooms, premium event halls, and excellent customer service for weddings, birthdays, conferences, and vacations.
